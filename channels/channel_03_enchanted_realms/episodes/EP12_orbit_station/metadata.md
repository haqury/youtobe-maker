### Description

The night shift is deep and almost no one is awake, and you are off duty and safe. You wander the dim amber corridors of a quiet space station to a round lounge, where a deep, even hum lives in the walls and a wide panoramic window looks down on a slow, cloudy planet. Rain shimmers faintly on its night side as it turns, and a heavy blanket and a small warm lamp wait beside your chair.

A slow, quiet second-person sci-fi story for a calm evening, told in simple, softly paced prose that grows shorter and quieter toward the end. Listen at low volume in a dim room, and let the hum, the blanket, the lamp and the turning world keep you company. Made for anyone who loves cozy science fiction, space station ambience, deep hum and rainy nights.
