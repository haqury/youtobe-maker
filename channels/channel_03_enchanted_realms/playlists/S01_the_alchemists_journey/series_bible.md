# The Alchemist's Journey

_RU: Путешествие алхимика_

## World and tone
The world is a dualistic fantasy realm where high magic is the sole province of the elite, while the forgotten dwell in the harsh, cobbled 'Beggars' Quarter.' The tone is profoundly atmospheric, tranquil, and 'dreamlike,' emphasizing the rhythmic bubbling of cauldrons, the scratching of quills, and the soft amber radiance of alchemical fires. The narrative bridges modern scientific logic with ancient mystical laws. The pacing is slow and meditative, crafted to immerse the audience in a world of steam, herbs, and luminous minerals.

## Characters (visual lock)
Elian: A young man with an old soul, pale skin, tousled dark hair, and striking silver eyes. He wears patched linen tunics or ink-stained leather aprons. Master Thorn: The grey-haired, stoic head of the Alchemists' School, with a long braided beard and intricate multi-lensed spectacles.

## Lore and rules
The world's logic is rooted in the application of earthly chemistry to magic, the struggle against poverty, and the defiance of an elite Academy. The visual style is 'Dark Academia meets Cozy Fantasy.' High-contrast scenes feature deep shadows and the warm flicker of candlelight. The focus remains on close-ups of hands mixing powders and bubbling flasks. The color palette is defined by deep browns, forest greens, and the golden shimmer of transmutation.
